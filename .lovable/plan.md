# Trinova Wellness Centre Website Rebuild

## Goal
Rebuild Trinova as a complete, polished healthcare website that feels calm, credible, and distinctly human. Preserve verified business information and service facts from the current site and brochures, remove duplicate or generic copy, and create a clear path from concern to appropriate care.

## Visual direction
- Refine the existing teal, deep blue, white, and restrained gold identity from the brochures.
- Use editorial serif headlines with a clean sans-serif body for an elevated, approachable feel.
- Favor generous space, crisp rules, strong typography, real human imagery, and quiet motion over decorative effects.
- Keep layouts direct and skimmable, with no generic feature grids, oversized pills, nested cards, or dense text walls.
- Use concise copy without em dashes.

## Site structure
- **Home:** Root-cause positioning, care categories, integrated-care model, recovery process, selected services, patient feedback, team introduction, location, and consultation CTA.
- **About:** Mission, Jennifer Anthony’s founder story and credentials, care principles, team roster, and four-step wellness process.
- **Services:** A clear directory grouped into mental health, pain and mobility, and nutrition and wellness.
- **Service detail pages:** Dedicated, internally linked pages for the verified clinical services, each with what it supports, what to expect, practitioner context, related care, and consultation CTA.
- **Balance Program:** Focused program page with verified inclusions, $3,300 tax-inclusive price, duration language clarified without unsupported promises, FAQ, and enquiry CTA.
- **Referral:** Referral benefits, eligible programs, reward tiers, three-step process, privacy note, and working referral form interaction.
- **Contact:** Phone, address, consultation options, service selector, map link, hours only if verified, and an accessible enquiry form.
- **Insights:** A clean index for the existing article content, with unique article pages where enough verified source content is available.

## Content and trust
- Use only claims, credentials, pricing, testimonials, and contact details verified from the supplied sources.
- Correct current duplicate service descriptions and spelling errors.
- Resolve the Balance Program versus Gut Reset naming conflict by using “Balance Program” consistently while describing nutrition and gut-health goals accurately.
- Avoid invented outcomes, fabricated statistics, unsupported medical promises, or invented opening hours.
- Add clear care-scope language and retain appropriate consultation-first wording.

## Interaction and accessibility
- Responsive navigation with an accessible mobile menu and persistent consultation action.
- Functional service filtering, expandable FAQs, referral/contact form validation, click-to-call, and map directions.
- Strong focus states, keyboard access, semantic headings, useful image descriptions, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and mobile-safe sizing.
- Forms will provide a complete front-end confirmation experience. Submission delivery will remain unconnected until a destination or backend is provided.

## SEO implementation
- Unique title, description, Open Graph metadata, canonical URL, and Twitter card metadata on every public page.
- LocalBusiness/MedicalClinic structured data using the verified Brampton address and phone number.
- Service and breadcrumb structured data where appropriate, plus Article data for insight pages.
- One clear H1 per page, descriptive internal links, local Brampton language used naturally, semantic content hierarchy, and crawlable route-based navigation.
- Preserve permissive crawler access. Defer a production sitemap URL until the public domain is connected.

## Technical approach
- Build reusable site navigation, footer, section headings, service layouts, calls to action, forms, and structured-data helpers.
- Keep service and team content in typed shared data so names, credentials, and descriptions remain consistent across pages.
- Generate a cohesive set of original wellness imagery rather than relying on generic stock photography.
- Validate the final experience at desktop and mobile widths, including menus, forms, links, metadata, and overflow.

## Assumptions and boundaries
- The supplied website and brochures are the source of truth where facts agree.
- No database, booking provider, email delivery, payment processing, or private patient-data collection will be added without the required service details.
- Sensitive health information will not be requested through the general contact or referral forms.
